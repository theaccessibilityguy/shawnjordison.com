Shawn Jordison is The Accessibility Guy, an accessibility educator and speaker. His experience spans college disability services, faculty teaching, accessibility consulting, and practical instruction for professionals. Through presentations, tutorials, and conversations, he helps people understand digital accessibility and apply it in their everyday work.
