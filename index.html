Shawn Jordison is an accessibility educator, speaker, and founder of The Accessibility Guy. His experience spans college disability services, faculty teaching, accessibility consulting, and practical instruction for professionals. Through presentations, tutorials, and conversations, he helps people understand digital accessibility and apply it in their everyday work.
